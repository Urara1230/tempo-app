Third-party notices for Tempo
=============================

The look of some controls in css/tempo.css (and the search icon in Tempo.html) is adapted from
elements published on Uiverse.io (https://uiverse.io, repository https://github.com/uiverse-io/galaxy).
They were re-done with Tempo's own colors, sizes and markup; the rules in tempo.css name their model.

  Element (galaxy path)                        Author        Used for
  Buttons/ErzenXz_slimy-chicken-63             ErzenXz       buttons, selects
  Radio-buttons/Yaya12085_rude-mouse-79        Yaya12085     view / scale switches (segmented control)
  Toggle-switches/adamgiebl_grumpy-moth-36     adamgiebl     the on / off switch
  Checkboxes/PriyanshuGupta28_quick-wolverine-69  PriyanshuGupta28  checkboxes (the box that turns into the tick)
  Inputs/escannord_hot-yak-44                  escannord     search box with the magnifier icon
  loaders/Fernando-sv_quiet-zebra-41           Fernando-sv   loading ring
  Checkboxes/Jack17432_shaggy-earwig-15        Jack17432     the three lines that turn into a cross (settings button)
  Cards/emmanuelh-dev_ancient-pig-83           emmanuelh-dev  the card with three dots (note field in the task panel)
  Toggle-switches/Cksunandh_massive-firefox-41 Cksunandh     the switch with a padlock on its knob (edit lock of shared projects)
  loaders/ClawHack1_evil-panda-55              ClawHack1     the swelling one after another (the title in the top bar, on hover)
